The three problems

1 · The shared parser reads dates month first · #1

Every tenant on file writes the day first, and the shared parser reads every date month first, so day and month swap: “4 March turned into 3 April”. It hits every tenant, through the app, by email and through the booking-v2 form. This is the problem #4471 describes.

2 · The booking-v3 form hints MM/DD · #2

All of these reports came through the booking form of the three tenants on booking-v3 (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour). The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type the month first, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The reports look like the parser’s, but the cause is the hint. A parser fix does not touch it. The table above marks this row “not this ticket — no ticket of its own”: its ticket is now #2.

3 · Dates move by one day · #3

These reports name no swapped month. The date moves by one day: “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May”, “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday”, “Every booking in June is one day off”, “The invite arrived before I made the booking”. That is a time-zone or day-boundary bug. The desk lists them as one-day moves (desk notes) and files them under the parser by tenant, but a parser fix will not close them.

Why they ended up in one ticket

  • Decision 0001 merges on the subject line. The same subjects, “Date is wrong” among them, occur in all three problems, so all three landed in #4471.
  • The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser; the table above shows how often it disagrees with the cause. It cannot split the ticket.
  • The split uses what the desk knows about the tenant (locale and booking form), the channel, and the desk’s own notes on one-day moves.

What to do

  • Track each problem in its own ticket: #1, #2, #3.
  • Change the status of #4471 from known to split, and point it to this page.
  • Before the next merge on subject, check the tenant and the channel: two reports with the same subject can have different causes.
